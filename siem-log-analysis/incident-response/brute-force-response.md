# Brute Force Response

## Detection
Multiple failed logins followed by successful authentication.

## Triage
Validate source IP, account, host and timestamps.

## Containment
Depending on confirmed risk, disable or protect the affected account and block malicious infrastructure according to organizational policy.

## Eradication
Remove persistence or unauthorized access if found.

## Recovery
Restore normal access, reset credentials where required and monitor.

## Documentation
Record evidence, actions and final conclusion.
